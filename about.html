About Foundable

A simple place for students across the university to report, search and claim lost valuables, so nothing important stays lost for long.

Our mission

Losing a wallet, a water bottle or a set of keys on campus is stressful. Finding it again should not be.

Foundable brings the university community together. Students who find an item post it with a photo, and students who lose something can search, report and claim it in a few easy steps.

How it works

Three simple steps to get a lost item back to its owner.

Report

Lost something or found something? Fill in a short form with the location, time and a description.

Search

Browse the list of found items or search by keyword to see if someone has already handed yours in.

Claim

Enter the item code and give a proof that it is yours, then get it back safely.

What we care about

The ideas behind how Foundable works.

Community first

Students helping students, by returning what is not theirs.

Safe returns

Proof is checked before an item is handed over to a claimant.

Quick and simple

Short forms and clear item cards so reporting only takes a minute.

Open and honest

Every found and lost item is listed in the open for everyone to see.
