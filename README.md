# Foundable-RuhunaUniversity-Group03
🔍 **Lost It? Found It? Let's Bring It Back!** 🎓

Welcome to the **University of Ruhuna Lost & Found Platform** — a smarter way to reunite lost belongings with their rightful owners!

Designed to make campus life easier, this platform provides a central place for students, lecturers, and university staff to report missing belongings, discover found items, and help others recover what they've lost.

From forgotten books and misplaced ID cards to lost bags and electronic devices, we're bringing the university community together to make finding lost belongings simpler, faster, and more convenient.

✨ **One Campus. One Community. Every Lost Item Deserves to Find Its Way Home.**

🛠️ Built with HTML, CSS.
